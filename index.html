A little about my journey

I haven't always lived with the clarity I speak about today.

I'm Arti Thakur — a mother, a seeker, and a Life & Manifestation Coach.

In 2019, after losing my father, I experienced grief, anxiety and moments of panic. Through my Guru's teachings, gratitude and a shift in perspective, I slowly learnt to navigate my pain differently — without pretending it wasn't there.

For more than six years, I also carried a deep longing to become a mother. That journey taught me faith, patience and the meaning of surrender.

These experiences changed the way I looked at life, my desires and, most importantly, myself. I realised transformation begins when we stop seeing ourselves as someone who must always come last — and start embracing the woman who knows her worth and consciously chooses herself, too.

Today, that's the shift I help other women explore.

Because I've been through uncertainty, longing and the search for a different way forward. And I believe you can create a life that feels more fulfilling without loving your family any less.

The heart of the philosophy

What does it mean to live in your Queen Energy?

It isn't about being perfect, having everything under control or putting yourself above others. It is about knowing your worth, honouring your desires, trusting your inner power and consciously choosing how you want to live.

As you begin to include yourself in the love and care you give so freely, that renewed energy can ripple into your children, your partner, your closest relationships and the atmosphere of your home.
